# 🐾 PetNutri — AI-Powered Pet Nutrition System

Welcome to **PetNutri**, an end-to-end decision support and personalized nutritional platform engineered for pet health optimization. By combining specialized veterinary knowledge bases with a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) backend, the system delivers precise ingredient safety evaluations, dynamic meal adjustments, and evidence-based dietary insights.

## 🎯 Key Capabilities

- **Interactive RAG Consultant:** Real-time query system powered by semantic vector retrieval over domain-specific pet nutrition documents.
- **Ingredient Safety & Nutrient Analysis:** Instant breakdowns covering essential dietary metrics (e.g., Antioxidants, Fiber) and portion limits.
- **Daily Health Logging:** Progress tracking interface for pet hydration, activity, and nutrition goals.
- **Grounding & Accuracy:** Built with localized vector indexing (ChromaDB) to eliminate LLM hallucinations and align with veterinary standards.
